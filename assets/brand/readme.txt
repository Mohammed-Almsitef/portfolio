MA monogram — Mohammed Almsitef

For a robotics and AI engineer. The M is a linkage: four bold links joined
at five pierced pivots, which read both as the joints of a robot arm and as
the nodes of a network. The A is a heading arrow, the marker a robot's pose
is drawn with on a map. Constructed on one grid, not taken from a typeface.

Grid: 120 units tall, 222 wide.
  M      links through (16,108) (16,12) (62,84) (108,12) (108,108),
         stroke 24, round joins and caps; a pivot hole of radius 5.5 at
         each of the five points
  arrow  M124 120 L172 0 L220 120 L172 92 Z (filled)
Small sizes: stroke 25 and pivots 5 at ~24px; stroke 26 and no pivots at 16px.
