MA monogram — Mohammed Almsitef

For a robotics and AI engineer. Two bold, forward-leaning letters cut from
one block: the M's last stroke and the A's first run parallel, separated by
a single slash. The M carries four pierced pivots (the joints of a linkage);
the A's counter is a round core (a node, a sensor). Constructed on one grid,
not taken from a typeface.

Grid: 120 units tall, 255.6 wide. Slants (x per unit of height): stem 0.15,
shared diagonal 0.55, the A's right leg 0.28. Slash width: 10.

     (70,120); stroke (70,120)(136,0)(170,0)(104,120)
  A  outer (114,120)(180,0)(222,0)(255.6,120); opening (146,120)(161.4,92)
     (215.8,92)(223.6,120); core centre (194.5,57) radius 7.5
  pivots, radius 5.2: (16.1,106) (29.9,14) (94.7,106) (145.3,14)
Small sizes: core only at ~24px; plain letters (triangular counter
(197.4,26.5)(209.6,70)(173.5,70)) at 16px.
