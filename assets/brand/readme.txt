MA ligature — Mohammed Almsitef

Construction (120-unit grid, one stroke weight, round caps and joins):
  M        M26 94 V30 L52 70 L78 30        (ink)
  A        M78 30 L98 94                   (accent)
  crossbar M52 70 H90.5                    (accent)
The M's second diagonal is the A's left leg, and the crossbar starts at the
M's valley, so the two letters share one construction.

Stroke: 12 at 48px+, 13 at ~32px, 14.5 at ~24px, 16 at 16px.
ViewBox: the mark's bounds plus half a stroke + 2 units of clearance.
